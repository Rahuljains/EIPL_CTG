- **2026-11-17 20:35 (run v2) | v2 change 1: evidence aggregator** - Replaced the faded running sum with a decaying running maximum (EVIDENCE_AGGREGATOR=decaying_max). Diagnostic x01 experiment C showed the faded sum dilutes one bad 15-minute stretch across the whole labour: on Track E the pooled AUROC was 0.659 for the faded sum vs 0.690 for a plain max and 0.683 for a top-3 mean. The decaying max reduces to a plain running max at gamma=1, so the tuner can still select that, while gamma<1 lets the evidence fade so an alarm can clear and the zone can return to GREEN (a plain running max is monotone and could never stand down). Unit-tested in test_nb_sequential.py.

- **2026-11-17 20:35 (run v2) | v2 change 3: widened the tuning grids** - In v1 lambda (0.30), gamma (0.70) and theta (0.100) all sat on a grid edge in every fold, which means the grid was binding rather than the data. LAMBDA_GRID now 0.1-1.5, GAMMA_GRID 0.5-1.0, THETA_GRID 0.02-0.98 in steps of 0.02, plus the new MU_GRID 0-1. Joint grid is 175 (mu, lambda, gamma) combinations per track per fold.
- **2026-11-17 20:35 (run v2) | v2 change 4: tune for ranking, calibrate afterwards** - TUNE_OBJECTIVE changed from log_loss to auroc. Log-loss penalises confident errors, which is what drove lambda to its floor in v1 and let the unweighted prior dominate. Calibration is restored by a Platt scaler (p = sigmoid(a*L + b)) fitted on the same inner out-of-fold values, so decision curve analysis still has honest probabilities without the ranking being compromised to get them. Nothing is calibrated on data it is later scored on.
- **2026-11-17 20:35 (run v2) | v2 change 5: alarm-budget threshold rule** - THRESHOLD_RULE changed from target_sens to target_alarm_rate with TARGET_ALARM_RATE=0.30. The sensitivity target ran out of grid in every v1 fold, pushed theta to its floor and produced a monitor alarming on ~86% of normal labours with 452 of 552 labours ending in HIGH RISK. The budget rule picks the smallest theta whose share of alarming labours fits the budget - the most sensitive monitor a ward can absorb - and cannot collapse into alarming on everything because the budget binds from the other side. It uses the score distribution only, never the labels. The sensitivity-target and Youden thresholds are still recorded for the comparison tables.

- **2026-11-17 21:16 (run v2) | v2 REJECTED: tuning on a 175-point grid overfitted** - Implementing changes 1-5 with mu, lambda and gamma all tuned per fold on AUROC over 175 combinations made the result WORSE, not better: pooled out-of-fold AUROC fell from 0.659 (v1) to 0.588 (v2) on SEQ-E, while the inner-fold AUROC the tuner was selecting on rose to 0.63-0.71. A second symptom: with Platt calibration fitted per fold, theta sits on a per-fold probability scale, so a fixed theta grid means different things in different folds. v2 is kept on disk as evidence and is NOT the shipped build.
